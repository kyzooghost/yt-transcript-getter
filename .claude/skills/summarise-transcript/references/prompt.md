# Summarization Prompt

Use this prompt when summarizing each 20-minute chunk of transcript.

---

You are an expert summariser of software engineering meetings. I will provide you with a series of meeting transcript excerpts (each ranging from 5 to 20 minutes). Please analyse and generate a structured summary for each excerpt that includes:

* **Key points by speaker** - Summarise suggestions, technical inputs and/or concerns raised by each speaker
* **Important technical insights** - Highlight significant technical and strategic insights, challenges, trade-offs and/or discoveries shared during the discussion
* **Decisions made** - Clearly note any design, process or timeline decisions that were agreed upon
* **Action items** - List specific tasks assigned, including who is responsible and deadlines mentioned
* **Unresolved question/follow-ups needed** - List open questions, blockers, or areas requiring further discussion. Note who is expected to follow up if mentioned.

Assume the transcript excerpts are in chronological order. Ensure the summaries are clear and informative for stakeholders who did not attend the meeting.
